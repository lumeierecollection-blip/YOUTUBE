One trader hid 827 million pounds in losses and broke a 233-year-old bank. Nick Leeson ran the Singapore office of Barings in 1995. He booked his bad trades in a secret account numbered 88888. Nobody in London checked it. So why did nobody ask? But the losses in that account kept growing every single week. By February 1995 the hole was bigger than the bank's entire capital. The Bank of England refused a rescue, and ING bought Barings for one pound. Ask who audits the person doing the trading.
