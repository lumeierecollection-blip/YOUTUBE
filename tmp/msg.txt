Board 38330253047 ch-49 beat 5 failed naming-check:

  "OTHER" should be "Other Mommy" (Incomplete title shown)
  "27.5 MILLION DOLLAR OPENING OF OTHER" - "The title on screen is incomplete;
   it is missing the word 'Mommy'." (second look)

The beat is a stat card whose LABEL reads "27.5 million dollar opening of Other
Mommy". fitText() laid that out in more lines than the label box has room for,
and the frame clipped the tail. "Mommy" was not crowded out of the layout - it
was cut off the end of it.

Both fitters had the same shape. After the band loop they returned whatever the
floor produced:

  return { size: min, lines: wrap(words, min, ...) };     // may be TALLER than maxLines

The floor is a size BAND preference - a label that small stops reading as a
label. It is not a licence to clip. Both fitText (canvas-layout.js) and
fitHeadline (typography.js) now keep stepping down below the floor until the text
fits its box, and give up only at MIN_FIT_FLOOR, where type stops being legible
at all. Text that already fits is untouched: the band loop still runs first, so
nothing shrinks that did not have to.

No gate is loosened. Nothing here can turn a failing beat green by ignoring it -
the fitter now returns text that actually fits instead of text that gets cut.
