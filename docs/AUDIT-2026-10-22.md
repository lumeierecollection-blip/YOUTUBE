## Root causes, ranked by impact

1. **Strict channels spend the whole script budget before asking whether the
   topic can be grounded at all.** The strict re-pick fires only at attempt 5
   of 8 (`daily-pipeline-v2.yml:869`), once per run, and the replacement topic
   gets 3 attempts. Nothing checks the *research* for drawable facts before
   the writer is asked; the SaaS-release / paper-method topics discovery keeps
   returning for ch-2/ch-48 can't be grounded without inventing (which the
   hard rules forbid), so the gate is correct and the loop is wasted.
2. **SCR-18 false positives feed noise into every re-ask** — capitalised runs
   that are a sentence-initial adverb + a real name ("Then Matt Graney") or a
   real name + a calendar word ("Scientific Reports October") are reported as
   names absent from research (`scripts/gate-script.js:170-198`, `NAME_LEAD`
   lacks adverbs; no calendar handling).
3. **References are never interpreted, only measured.** `derive-clone-grammar`
   and `lib/novelty.mjs` measure cadence (edges, not meaning); the planner gets
   4 evenly spaced stills (`reference-frames.js`), and only when Gemini
   answers (Groq/Ollama fallbacks plan blind, `gemini-visual-plan.js:893,910`);
   the whole-video critic receives **no** reference frames
   (`gemini-frame-review.js:426 const refFrames = []`). No structured
   creative-direction document or storyboard is produced from any reference.
   Per-channel signals contradict: `style-spec.json` reference reels for
   ch-05/06/08/10 are not among the 9 videos the laws were derived from.
4. **The repair loop only repairs one kind of failure.** `MAX_CORRECTION_LOOPS
   = 3` (`render-and-qa.js:126`) re-plans only after a frame-review reject;
   deterministic Layer-1 failures (canvas-fit, pop-transitions, entity-coverage
   …) go straight to `backupAudit` with no repair, though their ids are
   machine-readable. Defects have no shared schema and key on beat index.
   Aspect ratio / resolution are never checked; clone-cadence laws are gated
   only in `layout-proof.yml`, not production.
5. **Scene plans carry no direction rationale.** Per beat the plan has
   `scene_description`, `visual_type`, entities, layout, shot, camera — but no
   narrative purpose, transition intent, sound-cue intent, acceptance criteria
   or stable scene id, so QA can't test "did this scene do its job".
6. **Not resumable, not isolated at render.** Every run rediscovers,
   re-researches, rewrites, re-voices, re-renders. `render needs: prep` is a
   matrix-wide dependency: each channel's render waits for the slowest prep
   leg. Job state lives only in a 1-day `prep-status` artifact.
7. **OAuth-missing fails the render job after the render.** "Materialize OAuth
   credentials" exits 1 when a channel's secrets are absent
   (`daily-pipeline-v2.yml:1511-1513`); the deliverable survives in the queue
   but the job is red. `scripts/youtube-upload.cjs:210` still hard-codes a
   stale `[1,2,9,26,44,48]` publish list (run.js correctly reads
   `priority-channels.json`). Known credential state: ch-9/26 `invalid_client`,
   ch-44 `invalid_grant` — owner action.
