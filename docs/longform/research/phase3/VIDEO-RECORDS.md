# Phase 3 per-video evidence records

Generated by `output/longform-research/gen_records.py` from measured data. **Nothing here is invented.** Each record states how it was measured. Category shares are from labels a person (this session's model) assigned by looking at 240-px contact-sheet tiles: they are visual judgements, not detector output, and carry classification uncertainty (provenance such as stock vs commissioned cannot be read from a frame). Method limits are repeated in each section.

Sampling: one frame per 15 s of the analysed span. 'S' = share of sampled frames. 'W' = segment-weighted estimate of screen time (see `aggregate.py` in the research workspace; it is an estimate, never an exact duration). Shot rhythm uses two detectors: the hard-cut detector (misses dissolves) and a dissolve-aware detector validated on one window (about +-15%).
