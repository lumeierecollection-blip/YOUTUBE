# Competitor research, phase 2 (collected 13 November 2026)

Scope: 24 reference channels. The original 20 fixed videos remain the visual benchmarks for Hidden Past, AI Tested, Court & Spotlight and Rival Lines; this phase adds catalogue-level topic and thumbnail research for those four channels (their reference channels plus Johnny Harris, Matt Wolfe, Tifo Football and Law&Crime for editorial range) and new competitor discovery for Space, Psychology and Celebrity News.

**How channels were chosen.** Flat YouTube searches on 6 niche queries each (sorted by views all-time, this year, and relevance; `discover.sh`), ranked by how often a channel appeared among long videos; then judged by hand for fit (faceless or documentary/explainer long form, English, subject match). Not chosen just for size: Kurzgesagt is broad science, kept because space is its biggest category; TED/CrashCourse excluded as lecture formats; Nicki Swift and The List kept *because* their collapse is itself a finding.

**What was collected per channel** (`catalog.sh`, `enrich.mjs`): the newest up to 250 long videos (Shorts excluded) with views, durations and titles; upload date, likes and subscriber count for the top 5 and newest 5; uploads per month; and the median views of the newest 30, used to express every video as a multiple of its own channel's normal (`x_median`). Old videos' lifetime views are therefore not compared with new ones directly.

**Videos studied in content** (frames + local transcript of the first 8 and last 2 minutes, `study2.sh`): 16 new — Astrum *Uranus*, The Space Race *What NASA Found on Io*, SEA *The Cosmic Scale*, History of the Universe *What Is Beyond the Edge?*, Kurzgesagt *Largest Black Hole* (transcript only; frames failed), ElderFox *Mars in 4K*, Sprouts *The Pygmalion Effect*, PsychToons *Psychology of Discipline*, Psych2Go *Weird Habits… High Intelligence*, Practical Psychology *The Asch Line Study*, Aperture *Every Level of Consciousness*, Patrick Cc: *The Awakening of Jim Carrey*, SWOOP *The Downfall of Katy Perry*, Nicki Swift *News Anchors Who Vanished*, As Told by JP *Beyoncé Has Us All Fooled*, The List *Christian Siriano's Red Carpet Dresses* — plus the 20 fixed videos from phase 1.

**How they choose subjects (observed patterns, not their stated policy):**
- Space: a single named object with a new image or measurement ("We found…", "What NASA found on…"), or an absolute ("largest", "edge", "beginning"). Recency is a hook, the object is evergreen.
- Psychology: a famous named idea or experiment that viewers half-know; the video promises the real story or how it applies to them.
- Celebrity: a person already in the news cycle or a long-running reputation question; the angle is a verdict ("has us all fooled", "the downfall of", "fakest show"). Music explainers pick stars with long documented careers.
- Geopolitics/history: a current conflict explained through a map; "why X is empty/shaped/like this" evergreen geography.
- AI: a new model or tool the same week, with a demo; football: a manager or match of the week with a tactical thesis.

**Hooks (from the transcripts):** Astrum opens on what we cannot see and the single visit ("we have only ever visited Uranus once"); ElderFox states authenticity first ("The images in this video are all real"); PsychToons opens in the second person with a scenario ("There are about 40 seconds every morning that quietly decide your entire day"); Practical Psychology starts from a proverb it then contradicts; Patrick Cc: opens on the thesis ("he realized it was all a lie"); SWOOP opens on the subject's own clips; Nicki Swift opens on a one-line irony; As Told by JP opens on a reversal of the public image.

**Footage they use, and what that means for us:** Patrick Cc:, SWOOP, As Told by JP and Nicki Swift build their pictures from film, TV, music-video and news clips used under commentary/fair-use reasoning, plus the host on camera (Patrick Cc:, SWOOP). Space channels use renders, NASA imagery and some licensed stock. Psychology channels draw their own illustrations. We do not use unlicensed clips; the Celebrity News and Court systems replace them with licensed photographs, document captures and recreated statements, and that is the main visible difference from these references.

## Thumbnail language
Per-channel observations: `thumbnail-language.json`; colour measurements per thumbnail (brightness, saturation, dark/bright share, palette): `thumbnail-stats.json`; boards: `boards/g-*.jpg`. Patterns across winners:
- One dominant subject, 1–4 words of text, one symbol (arrow, ring, stamp, price) — in every niche.
- Space: the object full-bleed + "THIS IS X" tracking caps (Astrum, The Space Race); scale ring + arrow + label (SEA, Kurzgesagt); real image + red arrow (ElderFox).
- Psychology: the concept's name as the title on an illustration of the concept (Sprouts); one figure singled out from a crowd (Aperture); one red word (PsychToons).
- Celebrity: 1–3 large real faces; opinion headline in yellow condensed type (As Told by JP); a recreated post with a date (Patrick Cc:); arrow to a document/screenshot (SWOOP); avoid the purple-stage cut-out grid of the collapsed list channels.
- Our thumbnails reproduce these principles with our own assets: see each channel's three concepts in the approval package.

## Research gaps (stated plainly)
- Upload dates are fetched only for the top 5, newest 5 and 30th newest video per channel; "uploads per month" is from that span.
- Catalogues cover the newest 250 long videos; older catalogue hits are missed for prolific channels (Law&Crime posts ~176/month).
- Topic categories are keyword rules on titles: 30–39% of titles stay uncategorised; categories were checked by reading titles, not by watching.
- Only one video per new channel was studied in content; audio was not analysed for the new references.
- Views of videos newer than about 2 days show as 0 in flat listings (marked "0k").
- The Celebrity News "channel" has no YouTube channel of its own (see `../CHANNEL-AUDIT.md`).
