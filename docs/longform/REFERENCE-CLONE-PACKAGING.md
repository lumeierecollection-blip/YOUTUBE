## 1. Titles (the reference's title pattern, our topic)

What the references show:
- intrcpt: 5 of its top 12 long videos are "Why Do/Are ...?" questions; its four lowest are single-player opinion statements (`channel-reverse/reports/intrcptProductions.md`).
- Epic History: "<Series>: <Battle> <Year>" for campaign videos ("Napoleonic Wars: Battle of Austerlitz 1805"), and question-superlatives for single figures.

| | Primary | Alternates for a title test |
|---|---|---|
| Football | **Why Do Home Teams Win So Much?** | "Is Home Advantage Real? (We Checked 12,704 Matches)"; "Why Is Playing At Home Such An Advantage?" |
| History | **Norman Conquest: Battle of Hastings 1066** | "Battle of Hastings 1066: How England Was Conquered"; "Harold vs William: The Battle of Hastings 1066" |

Rejected: any title that claims what the evidence does not settle (e.g. "Referees Are Biased Towards Home Teams"), and "England's Last Stand" (an interpretation, not a sourced fact).

## 2. Thumbnails (measured formula, our art)

- intrcpt, 5 of 5 hits: white-to-paper gradient, lowercase serif headline with one bold word, ONE subject cut out of its photo with a motion-blur trail,
  a hand-drawn red arrow, a tiny mark top-left, a small italic aside. Both flops break it (a dark face photo; a dark bell curve).
  Ours: Fulham players at Craven Cottage (CC0 photo, background removed with rembg on GitHub, edge eroded 2 px), "home advantage." / "(is it real?)".
  Alternates: "45.6% at home." and "the 12th man.". Honest note: the photo is from Fulham v Everton, 10 May 2025, which Fulham lost 1-3 (it is in the credit).
- Epic, every thumbnail including the flops: torn red paper strip, huge condensed caps, a boxed series label, a year plate, a channel plate, a cut-out from period art.
  Ours: Horace Vernet's 1828 painting (public domain), the group cut out with rembg, "Battle of Hastings / HAROLD'S LAST DAY / 1066". Alternate: "Norman Conquest / HAROLD vs WILLIAM / 1066".
  Because Epic uses the same template on hits and flops, the thumbnail is not what separates their hits; for Hastings the test that matters is the title and topic.

Files: `output/longform-prod/<slug>/thumbnails/thumbnail-1-*.jpg` (primary) and -2, -3 (alternates) in each render artifact. Use YouTube's thumbnail test with all three.

Description openings (in `blurb`): intrcpt's pattern is a narrative paragraph, then "In this video...", then a data-source line, then a "no AI" line.
We cannot truthfully say "no AI" (the narration is a synthetic voice), so ours says: "The narration is a synthetic voice; no fact, figure or quote in this video is generated."
Epic opens with a one-line campaign summary, then sponsor, Patreon and music credits; ours keeps the summary and the credits and has no sponsor lines.

Pinned comment (both channels): the full source list is already in the description; pin one question that the video leaves open, in the reference's voice:
- Football: "In a two-legged tie, would you rather host the first leg or the second? (Evidence in the video: 2020/21 was the only season of 33 when away wins beat home wins.)"
- History: "The Bayeux Tapestry's caption 'Here King Harold has been killed' sits over two figures. Which one do you think is Harold?"

## 4. Posting strategy (what the reference data supports, and what it cannot)

| | intrcpt | Epic History TV |
|---|---|---|
| Upload time, hits | 14:00-19:45 UTC (Sat, Mon, Tue, Mon, Thu), 2026 | about 16:00 UTC, mostly Friday (3 of 6), Thu and Sat once each |
| Upload time, flops | 21:00 UTC (Tue, Wed), 2025 | 16:00 UTC Friday as well |
| Gap between long videos | 13 to 38 days among the 2026 hits | not measurable from this sample |
| Length | 16-25 min hits; 8.7 and 12 min flops | 17-53 min |

Plan:
1. Upload each video PRIVATE when the owner approves the preview; switch to public at the reference's slot:
   football **Thursday 17:30 UTC** (intrcpt's most recent hit slot, inside its 14:00-19:45 band), history **Friday 16:00 UTC** (Epic's most common slot).
2. Cadence: one long video per channel every 2-3 weeks (intrcpt's 13-38 day gaps), with one Short per long video in between as a funnel
   (the settled white/blue Shorts engine; the Short points to the long video).
3. After 48 hours and after 7 days, read CTR, first-30-second retention and average view duration (`analytics-branch.yml` on `upload/longform-16nov`),
   compare with the channel's previous long videos, and run the thumbnail test.
4. Limits of this plan, stated: 7 intrcpt and 6 Epic videos with timestamps; the flops are older than the hits (era and channel size confound the time-of-day signal);
   our channels are small, so the reference slot may not be our audience's slot. Treat the slot as the first test, not a rule.
