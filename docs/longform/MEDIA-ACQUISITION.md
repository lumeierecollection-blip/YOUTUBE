# Media acquisition workflow (long form)

Rule: an asset is usable only when its licence or permission is recorded. Being downloadable, embeddable or "on YouTube" is not permission.
No DRM, paywall or platform restriction is circumvented. Every asset gets a manifest row (generated by `platform/longform/v2/media-manifest.mjs`
into `docs/longform/episodes/<episode>/MEDIA-MANIFEST.json`): file, source URL, creator, licence, attribution, download date, channel, episode,
beats that use it, commercial reuse, editing, usable. The same script lists **footage gaps** (beats where the reference channel shows video we
cannot license) and **missing files**. An episode does not go to voice or render while a used asset is not `yes`.

## Football (Rival Lines)
- **Have, licensed:** StatsBomb open data (events, lineups, 360 freeze-frames; free with attribution to StatsBomb; **commercial publication terms to be confirmed with StatsBomb**), Commons/La Moncloa photographs of the squad, stadium and players.
- **Do not have:** any match footage, goals, broadcast tactical cams, press conferences or interviews. Football Made Simple and Tifo show such clips; we cannot without a licence.
- **Replacement used:** event-data animation of the actual moves (positions from 360 freeze-frames), licensed stills with credit, scorecards, shot maps.
- **To investigate before scaling (owner decision):** a sports-footage licence (agency or league licensee) and its cost; StatsBomb commercial terms; whether official club/UEFA press-conference video carries any reuse licence (assume no).

## Entertainment (Celebrity News, Court & Spotlight)
- **Have, licensed:** Commons photographs of the people (many CC BY / BY-SA event photos), official documents (court PDFs, government releases), public statements recreated as text with source and date.
- **Do not have:** red-carpet video, interviews, talk-show clips, music videos, concert footage, court video.
- **Replacement used:** real photographs with credit tags, document captures with highlighted lines, recreated public statements (words only, cited), timelines, ownership and money-flow diagrams.
- **Watch:** the newest celebrity photos on Commons are often from a few events; check each photo's date against the claim it illustrates (labels carry the photo date).

## Space (Red Space Facts)
- Richest legal source: NASA images and SVS visualisations are public domain; ESA/Webb images are often CC BY 4.0. Every picture carries a provenance badge: observation, visualisation, model, artist impression or hypothesis.
- Video: SVS provides the real footage tier (used: item 4666, two 96 s clips). Full clips stay outside git (`output/longform-media/`).

## Psychology
- Footage of historic experiments is usually copyrighted (archival film); we did not use any. Visuals are our own reconstructions from the published method, real data (Raudenbush 1984 effect sizes) and Commons portraits.
