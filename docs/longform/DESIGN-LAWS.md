# Long-form design laws, per channel (v2)

Source: `docs/longform/analysis/*.md` (20 fixed references; measured + frame-observed; audio NOT analysed; Diddy doc not transcribed). Every law below cites what was seen. This replaces the retired v1 system (`archive/longform-v1-rejected`). Nothing here is built yet.

## Hidden Past (RealLifeLore x5)
Seen: map-led films (Saudi, Russia, Canada, US density) hold ONE relief map and re-frame it; colour = ownership/route only; labels small uppercase on land; footage and period engravings are short evidence inserts; the Nepal film is footage-led instead. Opening is either an uncut dated clip with source credit (Nepal) or an uncut data map with labels arriving in narration order (US density, 0 cuts in 240 s).
Build: (1) `Relief map` base + camera re-frame, (2) ownership fill + route stroke, (3) choropleth with legend, (4) credited-footage plate, (5) period-engraving plate, (6) scale-translation card, (7) sponsor-free ending.
Sample subject: a geography-heavy subject with Commons/NASA/USGS assets. Candidate (not yet researched): "Why the Strait of Malacca decides Asian trade". Needs a research pass before any claim is written.

## AI Tested (AI Search x5)
Seen: demo-led. Real screen captures cropped and zoomed, highlight rectangle on the discussed line, benchmark charts with winner outlined, model pages/READMEs, side-by-side outputs with column titles, pill label naming the pair under test (game mash-ups). Opening = fast varied clips, then first real page by ~12 s.
Build: (1) page-capture with highlight box and push-in, (2) side-by-side output pair, (3) benchmark bar with outlined winner, (4) pill label, (5) prompt-and-result card showing the settings beside the output, (6) explainer flow diagram after the demo.
Sample subject: a real tool we can run ourselves (our own tests are the evidence). Candidate: "Do AI coding assistants follow a 10-rule spec?" Must be actually run and recorded before any claim.

## Court & Spotlight (SunnyV2 x3, Diddy doc, FRONTLINE)
Seen: commentary films are source-captured: article, forum post, post with blur-to-reveal, highlighted claim line, "Credit:" tag on every clip, white-box black-serif quote card, dated transcript headers on black. The two documentaries are interview plus mood B-roll with a persistent provenance bug ("Originally aired on") and no data graphics.
Build: (1) source capture with highlighted line and credit tag, (2) blur-reveal social post, (3) dated transcript card, (4) quote card, (5) timeline strip, (6) allegation vs finding ledger (our addition for the safety rule), (7) provenance bug.
Rules: real photographs only; allegation and finding never share a card. Sample subject must be an internationally recognised figure with a documented court record and public-domain/Commons photos; not chosen yet (needs a research pass; do not pick from memory).

## Rival Lines (Football Made Simple x5)
Seen: dark vignetted top-down pitch, dots (club colours, faces in some), big white number opener, scoreline card re-used as minute marker, broadcast clip with ring/beam on one player and ONE capital-letter callout, heat-map phone card, "COMING UP..." section tag, timeline spine for long-arc stories.
Build: (1) pitch plane + dot field, (2) scoreline/minute card, (3) clip-callout, (4) line overlay (back-line, forward trio), (5) heat-map card, (6) number opener, (7) timeline spine.
Sample subject: an international fixture or rivalry with open event data. Candidate (not yet checked): a StatsBomb open-data match; confirm which are published before choosing. Real photos/clips only if licensed; otherwise abstract dots.

- Sponsor reads sit after the argument (Nepal, Saudi, US density).
- Stills only until approved. No sampled frame is evidence of audio or motion easing.
