## What the comparison says

- **Length is the big gap.** The references are 14.5 and 30 minutes; ours are 4.5 and 3.3. The brief for this phase says 8 to 13 minutes where the material supports it and not to pad. Both of our episodes are built only from material we have actually sourced, so they stop where the sourced material stops.
- **To reach 8 minutes at the references' pace we would need about 1,200 words**, about twice the current script (606 and 482), and every added line would need a source from the same kind of record. That is a research job per episode, not a rendering job.
- **Pace**: the references speak at 155 and 180 words per minute; our synthetic voices measure about 145. The voice is never sped up (standing rule), so a faster feel has to come from tighter sentences and more cuts, not from speed.
- **Cuts**: AI Search cuts about every 4.4 s; we hold a scene for 10 to 25 s. More cuts need more distinct pictures per beat (more sourced stills, clips and real pages), which again is sourcing, not rendering.
- **Opening**: both of ours already follow the references' opening (result or real footage first, credit visible).

## Material that exists and is not yet used (for extending without padding)

- **Mount St. Helens** (both saved Wikipedia articles, `data/longform/hidden-past/msh/`): the ash column and the ash that fell on eleven states; the 'quiet zone' where the blast was not heard; Spirit Lake and the log mat; the animals, salmon and timber lost; the 'moonscape' remark by the president; the later eruptions of 1980 to 1991 and 2004 to 2008; plus the unused USGS photographs and four more clips from the National Archives film.
- **AI Tested**: three more stored tests with their data (honesty/unknown, privacy, letter counting, `data/longform/ai-tested/`), plus the real pages for the Faith and Fate paper and the model list.

Extending either episode with these would be new scripted beats, each tied to a source line, then re-rendered.
