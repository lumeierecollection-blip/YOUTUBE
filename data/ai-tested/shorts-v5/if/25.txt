Google is testing a tool where you ask an AI to build the app your group needs. It is called Tabletop. Your group shares one online space. Add links, files and ideas, then ask the agent, or pick a starter template. It can build things like these. First, a trip itinerary. Second, a dinner poll. Third, an expense splitter. Fourth, a tournament bracket. And here is the part that matters. Everyone uses it at the same time, like a Google Doc. It is still a Google Labs experiment. US only, adults eighteen and over, by waitlist. You just found the tool that builds your group's planning tools. Follow AI Tested.
