Claude can now turn an artifact into a Mac or Windows app. It is called Install as app, and it is in beta. The app gets its own icon, and opens in its own window instead of inside Claude. It can use Claude and your connectors, and it updates when the artifact is edited. Installed apps can send notifications, or sit in your menu bar. Anthropic people made apps like Linnet, a layered image editor, and Tern, a terminal with Claude beside you. You need to be online and signed in to open one.
