Rome made one farmer dictator for six months. He quit on day sixteen. In 458 BC, an enemy army had trapped a Roman army. The senate sent envoys to a four-acre field across the Tiber. They found Cincinnatus digging a ditch, or ploughing. They saluted him as Dictator. He surrounded the enemy, and they begged for mercy. He sent them under a yoke made of three spears. Then Livy says he resigned, on the sixteenth day, from an office meant to last six months. Six months of power. He took sixteen days.
