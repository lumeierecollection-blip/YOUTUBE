Rome made one farmer dictator for six months. He quit on day sixteen. In 458 BC, the Aequi trapped a Roman army. The senate sent envoys to Cincinnatus. He worked a four-acre field across the Tiber. They found him digging a ditch, or ploughing. The envoys saluted him as Dictator. The Aequi were attacked on both sides, and they begged for mercy. He sent them under a yoke of three spears. Livy says he resigned on the sixteenth day. The office was meant to last six months. Six months of power. He took sixteen days.
