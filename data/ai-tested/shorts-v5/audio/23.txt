Four nonprofits are asking a San Francisco court to stop next-generation AI. Their targets: Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI, and SpaceX. The ask: an emergency injunction until companies can prove humans stay in control. The complaint cites AI-driven cyberattacks and the companies' own admissions that AI could go rogue. Congress has yet to pass legislation governing AI. So the question isn't just can we build it. It's should a court let it keep going?
