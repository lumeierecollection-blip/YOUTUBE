BLOCKER (item 8): the planner chain is Gemini -> Ollama only; the Ollama
link is unavailable in CI, and the rule-based third link is not re-enabled.

What is wired (scripts/gemini-visual-plan.js, 2026-09-28):
  1. Gemini (gemini-3.5-flash-lite via src/lib/gemini-client.js — 3 keys
     rotated on 429/5xx; one uncached retry; JSON repairs).
  2. Ollama at OLLAMA_HOST (default http://127.0.0.1:11434), model
     OLLAMA_PLAN_MODEL (default qwen2.5:7b). Reached when Gemini leaves no
     beats (429, 503, timeout, malformed JSON after repair). Logged:
       [planner] gemini failed: <reason>, falling to ollama
       [planner] ollama failed: <reason> — no further fallback
  FORCE_PLANNER=ollama skips Gemini to exercise link 2.

Why link 2 does not work in CI today:
  - The RENDER job (where planning runs) never starts an Ollama server;
    only the PREP job does, with qwen2.5:3b.
  - qwen2.5:7b on the 4-vCPU runner could not finish one Discover call in
    the job budget (data/ci-runs/blocked-prep-model.txt); a full visual
    plan (up to ~16k output tokens) is far larger.
  - Verified locally: with no server the link returns
    "unavailable at http://127.0.0.1:59999 (ECONNREFUSED)" and exits 1.
  - So the FORCE_PLANNER=ollama CI test was NOT run: by construction it
    would report "unavailable" and fail the render.
