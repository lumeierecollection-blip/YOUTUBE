## Script (~75 words, ~26 s at v3's 172 wpm)
In 2022, Jake Moffatt's grandmother died. He asked Air Canada's chatbot about bereavement fares. It said: fly now, claim the discount within
90 days. He paid 1,630 dollars. Air Canada said no. Its defence? The chatbot is a separate legal entity, responsible for its own actions.
The tribunal called that remarkable, and made Air Canada pay. If your AI says it, you said it. Would you trust an airline's chatbot?
