# B-Roll Sourcing Guide — Per Niche

Research-backed B-roll sourcing for every niche in the 50-channel system.

---

## Universal B-Roll Rules

1. **Minimum 6-8 unique B-roll clips per minute** of video
2. **Hero shot**: 4-8 seconds (thumbnail candidate)
3. **Standard B-roll**: 2-4 seconds per clip
4. **Quick cuts**: 1-2 seconds during montages
5. **Data overlays**: 5-7 seconds (viewer needs time to read)
6. **Ken Burns on stills**: 4-8 seconds with slow zoom/pan
7. **Every cut must reinforce the exact point** being made
8. **Color grade all B-roll** to match video's overall look
9. **Sound bridges**: start next scene's audio 2-6 frames before visual cut
