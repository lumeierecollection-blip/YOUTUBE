They cut their rent from two thousand to eight hundred pounds, moving onto a narrowboat. Then came ten thousand pounds to stop it sinking.

In June, Daniel and Charlotte left their jobs and moved onto a three-bed narrowboat. Costs dropped from two thousand to eight hundred a month. Twelve hundred saved, every month.

The space is tight: three hundred square feet, a two-fifty-litre water tank, a toilet emptied by hand. And every fourteen days, the boat must move.

Then the boat began to sink. The fix: a completely new base, for ten thousand pounds. Suddenly, eight hundred a month looked small.

There's a joke among boaters: Boat stands for Bring Out Another Thousand. Follow Narrowboat Life, for the honest side of the water.
