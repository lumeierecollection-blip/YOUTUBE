The snowball versus avalanche debate is a $215 question. Not choosing at all costs you $13,600.

Avalanche pays the highest interest first. It wins the math. On a realistic $10,636 in debt, it finished in 27 months with $2,838 in interest. Snowball pays the smallest balance first. It took 28 months and $3,053. A gap of just $215.

But the calculators miss the point. A Kellogg study of 6,000 debt settlement clients found the people who actually finished were the ones who closed accounts early. The snowball closes its first account in month four. The avalanche closes nothing until month ten. Small wins keep people going.

So the real answer? Avalanche, if you trust your discipline. Snowball, if you've quit a plan before. Either beats paying minimums. Follow for the numbers.
