## PART 1: SCRIPT STRUCTURE

### Universal Script Framework

Every video follows this skeleton, adapted per niche:

```
[HOOK — 0:00-0:30]
- Deliver the payoff/visual FIRST, explain SECOND
- Use specific-result hooks (71% 30-sec retention)
- Never say "In this video we're going to talk about..."
- Show the most dramatic visual from the video

[SECTION 1 — 0:30-2:30]
- Context and background
- End on a question or tease that pulls into Section 2
- Pattern interrupt at 2:00 mark (new visual approach, surprising fact)

[SECTION 2 — 2:30-5:00]
- Deep dive into core topic
- Data overlays for key statistics
- B-roll is the PRIMARY teaching tool, not decoration
- Micro-loop at end of section (tease next section)

[SECTION 3 — 5:00-7:30]
- Escalation or investigation
- Reveal the key insight at 60-70% through video
- Strategic silence (750ms-1000ms) after major revelations

[CLOSE — 7:30-end]
- Resolution or broader implications
- Bookend technique: repeat hook phrase in final line
- CTA integrated naturally, not interrupting momentum
```

### Hook Formulas (Ranked by 30-Second Retention)

1. **Specific-result hook**: "A man lived 438 days without a heartbeat" — 71% avg retention
2. **Contrarian statement**: "Your front door lock is completely useless" — 65% avg retention
3. **Direct promise**: "By the end of this, you'll never look at bread the same way" — 62% avg retention
4. **Scale/wonder**: "There are more bacteria in your mouth than people on Earth" — 58% avg retention
5. **Question curiosity gap**: "Why is there a massive black ball floating in this reservoir?" — 55% avg retention

### Script Writing Rules

- **Write for the ear, not the eye** — short sentences, conversational rhythm
- **One idea per sentence** — no compound-complex constructions
- **Vivid sensory details** — paint the scene with specifics, not generics
- **First-person limited perspective** (for crime/mystery) — viewer only knows what the character knows
- **Statistics land with impact** — state the number, pause, let it sink in, then contextualize
- **Section transitions**: Use "Meanwhile..." / "What they didn't know yet was..." / "But here's where it gets interesting..."
- **Never pad** — if every minute doesn't add value, cut it

### Voiceover Pacing

| Niche Category | Base Pace | Pause Length | Notes |
|---------------|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Science/nature | 0.94x normal speech | 750-1000ms between paragraphs | Measured, authoritative |
| True crime | 0.90-0.94x | 1000-1500ms after reveals | Slow, deliberate, tension-building |
| Food/travel | 1.0x normal speech | 500-750ms | Energetic, enthusiastic |
| History | 0.94x | 750-1000ms | Measured, storytelling |
| Lifestyle/craft | 1.0x | 500ms | Conversational, relaxed |
| Education | 0.96x | 750ms | Clear, instructional |

## PART 2: VISUAL SYSTEM BY STYLE

### Style 1: Cinematic Documentary

**Channels using this**: ch-01, ch-03, ch-04, ch-06, ch-08, ch-10, ch-12, ch-14, ch-17, ch-19, ch-20, ch-21, ch-22, ch-25, ch-27, ch-28, ch-30, ch-34, ch-37, ch-39, ch-41, ch-42, ch-45, ch-46, ch-48

**Visual DNA**:
- Film grain overlay at 8-15% opacity (finer grain for modern, coarser for archival)
- Warm/cool color splits mark narrative progression (warm gold = nostalgia, cold steel = crisis)
- Subtle vignette at 15-25% opacity
- Ken Burns effect on stills: 0.5-2% zoom per second, always toward subject
- Slow zoom / dolly for establishing shots
- Depth/parallax layering for composites
- Dramatic pacing cuts: fast during crisis (0.5-1s), slow during reflection (3-4s)

**Camera Angles (per niche)**:
| Niche | Primary Angles | B-Roll Focus |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| Science/nature | Wide establishing → macro detail → data overlay | Expedition footage, lab work, nature B-roll |
| True crime | Evidence boards, document reveals, Ken Burns on photos | Court footage, news clips, location drone shots |
| Food/street food | Top-down (bird's eye), extreme close-up, beauty angle (45°) | Cooking close-ups, market footage, ingredient prep |
| Travel | Drone establishing, handheld intimate, over-shoulder | Street-level walking, landmark reveals, map animations |
| Military/abandoned | Wide exterior → detail decay → slow walkthrough | Location drone shots, detail close-ups, archival |
| Van life/family | Gimbal walkthrough, person-in-space, detail close-ups | Van interior, exterior in environment, daily routine |

**Color Grading by Niche**:
| Niche | Primary Grade | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| Science/space | Cool blues, deep blacks, high contrast | Cosmic feel, data-driven |
| True crime | Desaturated, cool/blue tones, near-black | Never bright saturated colors |
| Food | Warm, saturated, golden amber tones | Appetizing, inviting |
| Travel | Teal-and-orange cinematic | Warm skin tones, cool skies |
| Military/abandoned | Desaturated, gritty, film grain | Decay aesthetic |
| History | Sepia tones, film grain, paper texture | Period-appropriate |
| Van life | Natural, warm, slightly desaturated | Cozy, authentic |

**Transition SFX**:
- Whooshes: fast air sweeps for visual cuts
- Risers: upward-frequency builds before reveals
- Impact hits/sub-booms: deep thuds for chapter breaks
- Reversed cymbals: swell into transitions

### Style 2: Minimal / Kinetic Typography

**Channels using this**: ch-05→NEW, ch-07, ch-09, ch-15, ch-23→NEW, ch-24, ch-31, ch-35, ch-38, ch-44, ch-47

**Visual DNA**:
- Clean gradient backgrounds (no texture, no grain)
- Kinetic typography: text moves with purpose, appears/scales/transitions cleanly
- Simple flat icons animate to illustrate concepts
- Very gentle pan/zoom (restrained, never aggressive)
- Typography hierarchy: heading → subheading → body
- Full-screen type moments for emphasis (strong claim, statistic, punchline)

**Camera Angles**:
- Text-centric: full-screen text with animated reveals
- Icon-centric: flat icons animate to illustrate concepts
- Data-centric: clean charts and graphs build dynamically
- Minimal B-roll: used sparingly for context breaks

**Color Grading**:
- Clean, high-contrast
- Channel accent color for emphasis
- Dark backgrounds with light text (most niches)
- No film grain, no vignette

**Transition SFX**:
- Clean whooshes (shorter than cinematic)
- Digital glitch: brief static bursts for tech transitions
- Pop/bubble: organic pops for playful motion graphics
- Typewriter: mechanical keystroke rhythms for text

### Style 3: Motion Graphics

**Channels using this**: ch-11, ch-13, ch-16, ch-18, ch-26, ch-29, ch-32, ch-33, ch-36, ch-40, ch-43, ch-50

**Visual DNA**:
- Layered depth/parallax (multiple layers at different speeds)
- Icon scene compositions: full scenes from animated icons
- Bold color wipes between sections
- Data visualization animations (charts build dynamically)
- Character/icon animations that act out scenarios
- Smooth 60fps motion, geometric shapes, bold colors

**Camera Angles**:
- Animated camera movements (zoom in for detail, out for scale)
- Parallax layering for depth
- Scene-based: each scene is a mini-composition
- Data-first: numbers and charts are the visual

**Color Grading**:
- Bold, high-contrast color palettes
- Themed per video (cosmic blues for space, reds for biology)
- Clean gradients, smooth transitions
- No film grain, no desaturation

**Transition SFX**:
- Glass/crystal hits for icon appearances
- Soft chimes for completed infographics
- Spring/bounce for bouncy icon animations
- Digital typing for futuristic text animations

| Layer | Purpose | Volume (speaking) | Volume (silent) |
|-------|---------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Ambient bed** | Emotional foundation, sustained mood | -30dB to -35dB | -25dB to -30dB |
| **Music bed** | Narrative arc, emotional journey | -18dB to -25dB | -12dB to -15dB |
| **Spot SFX** | Punctual sounds timed to visuals | -20dB to -15dB | -12dB to -10dB |

### B-Roll Quantity Rules
- **Minimum 6-8 unique B-roll clips per minute** of video
- **Hero shot**: 4-8 seconds (the money shot, used for thumbnail candidate)
- **Standard B-roll**: 2-4 seconds per clip
- **Quick cuts**: 1-2 seconds during high-energy montages
- **Data overlays**: 5-7 seconds (viewer needs time to read)
- **Ken Burns on stills**: 4-8 seconds with slow zoom/pan

### B-Roll Editing Rules
- **Color grade all B-roll** to match video's overall look
- **Every cut to B-roll should reinforce the exact point** being made in that moment
- **Never use generic "filler" footage** — viewers mentally tag it
- **Match B-roll style to content**: nature docs need different pacing than tech explainers
- **Sound bridges**: start audio from next scene 2-6 frames before visual cut

## PART 5: THUMBNAIL SYSTEM

### Universal Thumbnail Rules

1. **1280x720 pixels** (16:9 aspect ratio)
2. **One clear subject with emotion** — cannot point at a single subject = too busy
3. **Max 5 words of text** — reducing from 7→4 words averages +34% CTR
4. **Bold sans-serif font** — Montserrat Extra Bold or similar
5. **Mobile-first**: must read at ~168x94 pixels
6. **Subject 30%+ brighter or darker** than background
7. **Warm subject, cool background** (orange/red against blue/teal)
8. **Keep bottom-right clear** (video duration timestamp covers this)
9. **Curiosity gap between title and thumbnail** — never repeat same words

### Thumbnail Patterns by Niche

| Niche | Pattern | Colors | Text |
|-------|---------|--------|------|
| Science | Dramatic visual + question | Cool blues, cosmic | "What If..." / "Why..." |
| True crime | One face + evidence element | Desaturated, dark | Case number, date, location |
| Food | Close-up food + reaction | Warm amber, golden | "World's Best..." / "I tried..." |
| Travel | Person in landscape | Teal-orange cinematic | Location + superlative |
| History | Archival photo + question | Sepia, film grain | Date, location, "The Lost..." |
| Lifestyle | Finished product/space | Clean, bright | Size, fact, "How to..." |
| Engineering | Scale comparison | High contrast, bold | Number + "vs" / comparison |
| Education | Concept illustration | Clean, bold colors | Question or surprising fact |

### CTR Benchmarks
- 1-2%: Low (needs redesign)
- 3-4%: Average
- 5-7%: Good/strong
- 8-10%: Excellent
- 10%+: Exceptional

### A/B Testing Protocol
- Use YouTube Studio's native A/B test
- Run for 7 days minimum before deciding
- If CTR is already 6%+, don't touch it
- As impressions expand, CTR naturally drops (12% at 1K → 5-6% at 100K)

## PART 6: PACING REFERENCE

### Shot Duration by Content Type

| Content Type | Avg Shot Length | Hero Shot | B-Roll |
|-------------|----------------|-----------|--------|
| Documentary (cinematic) | 5-8 seconds | 8-15 seconds | 3-5 seconds |
| Fast-paced explainer | 2-4 seconds | 4-6 seconds | 1-3 seconds |
| Data visualization | 4-6 seconds | 6-8 seconds | 3-4 seconds |
| Food close-ups | 4-6 seconds | 6-8 seconds | 2-3 seconds |
| Travel montage | 1-3 seconds | 5-8 seconds | 2-3 seconds |
| Craft/build process | 4-8 seconds | 6-10 seconds | 2-4 seconds |

### The Pacing Rollercoaster

```
FAST (hook) → SLOW (context) → BUILD (investigation) → FAST (revelation) → SLOW (reflection) → FAST (close)
```

### Retention Optimization
- **First 30 seconds**: Must include the hook — best visual from the video
- **2:00 mark**: Audience most engaged → sponsor sweet spot
- **7-9 minute mark**: Second engagement peak → key reveal
- **Minutes 2-7**: "Danger zone" — pacing must stay tight
- **Final 2 minutes**: Resolution, beauty shots, CTA

### Pattern Interrupts (Every 2-3 Minutes)
- New visual approach
- Surprising fact or statistic
- Demonstration or visual reveal
- Question that creates curiosity gap
- Brief chapter card / section divider
