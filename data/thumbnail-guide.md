## Design Principles

### 1. Readability at Small Size
- Thumbnail appears as small as 168x94px on mobile
- Text must be readable at this size — bold, large, high contrast
- Test: shrink to thumbnail size, can you read it in 0.5 seconds?

### 2. Emotional Hook
- Thumbnail should make you FEEL something before you read it
- Use facial expressions, dramatic imagery, or bold claims
- Match the emotional tone of the video (outrage, curiosity, fear, awe)

### 3. Contrast & Color Pop
- High contrast between elements (light/dark, warm/cool)
- Accent color that pops against the background
- Avoid muddy or low-contrast combinations

### 4. Simplicity
- Max 3-4 elements (text + image + accent)
- If everything is emphasized, nothing is
- White space is your friend

### 5. Consistency
- Same style per channel — recognizable in a feed
- Same fonts, same color palette, same layout structure
- Builds brand recognition over time

## Style-Specific Guidelines

### Cinematic Documentary (MagnatesMedia, ColdFusion, Company Man)

**Layout:**
- Dark/moody background (dark gradient or blurred image)
- One bold text element (company name, key figure, or dramatic word)
- Supporting visual (logo, person, or symbol related to topic)
- Gold/amber accent color for emphasis

**Colors:**
| Element | Hex | Usage |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Background | #0D1117 | Dark base |
| Secondary bg | #1A1F2E | Gradient |
| Accent | #C9A227 | Gold — key text, emphasis |
| Text primary | #FFFFFF | Main headline |
| Text secondary | #A0AEC0 | Subtitles |

**Typography:**
- Font: Inter, Montserrat, or similar bold sans-serif
- Weight: 900 (Black) for headline
- Size: Headline fills 40-60% of width
- Shadow: Subtle drop shadow for depth

**Imagery:**
- Company logos (clean, high-res)
- Person photos (high contrast, dramatic lighting)
- Symbolic imagery (money, buildings, charts)
- NO busy backgrounds — keep it clean

**Examples:**
- "TOYS R US" in gold on dark background with Geoffrey silhouette
- "$5.3 BILLION" in large white text with red accent
- Company logo faded in background, bold text overlay

### Minimal (Kurzgesagt, Vox, TED-Ed)

**Layout:**
- Clean solid or gradient background
- One clear icon or simple illustration
- Bold, clean text
- Minimal elements — maximum 3

**Colors:**
| Element | Hex | Usage |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Background | #1E293B | Dark blue-grey |
| Accent | #38BDF8 | Bright blue — pop |
| Text | #F8FAFC | Clean white |
| Secondary | #94A3B8 | Muted text |

**Typography:**
- Font: Inter, SF Pro, or system font
- Weight: 700-900
- Clean, no effects or shadows
- Centered or left-aligned

**Imagery:**
- Flat icons or simple illustrations
- No photos — use graphics
- Geometric shapes
- Clean lines

### Motion-Graphics (The Infographics Show, Half as Interesting)

**Layout:**
- Bold, colorful background
- Character or icon illustration
- Large text with impact
- High energy visual

**Colors:**
| Element | Hex | Usage |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Background | #1E3A5F | Bold blue |
| Secondary | #E8B931 | Yellow — energy |
| Accent | #E74C3C | Red — urgency |
| Text | #FFFFFF | Clean white |

**Typography:**
- Font: Impact, Bebas Neue, or similar display font
- Weight: Bold/Black
- Large, fills frame
- Outline or shadow for contrast

**Imagery:**
- Illustrated characters or icons
- Action-oriented poses
- Scene-based compositions
- High contrast, saturated colors

## Text on Thumbnails — Rules

### Word Count
- **Maximum 5 words** — less is more
- 3-4 words is ideal
- Every word must earn its place

### What to Write
| Type | Example | When to Use |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Company/Subject name | "TOYS R US" | Documentary about specific entity |
| Key figure | "$5.3 BILLION" | Financial drama |
| Dramatic claim | "DEAD IN 10 YEARS" | Downfall story |
| Question | "WHO KILLED IT?" | Mystery/investigation |
| Contrast | "FROM BILLION TO ZERO" | Transformation story |

### What NOT to Write
- Full sentences
- Clickbait not backed by video
- Vague phrases ("You Won't Believe")
- More than 5 words

### Typography Rules
- ALL CAPS for impact (or Title Case)
- Bold weight (700-900)
- High contrast against background
- Drop shadow if on busy background
- Consistent font per channel

## Image Selection Rules

### DO Use:
- High-resolution photos (min 800px wide)
- Relevant to video topic
- Dramatic/emotional expressions
- Clean, uncluttered backgrounds
- Iconic/symbolic imagery

### DON'T Use:
- Blurry or low-res images
- Generic stock photos
- Text-heavy images
- Multiple competing focal points
- Images that don't match video content (clickbait)

### Person Photos
- High contrast, dramatic lighting
- Looking toward camera or in action
- Cropped tight (face fills 30-50% of frame)
- No busy backgrounds behind them

### Company/Brand Photos
- Clean logo on solid/dark background
- Iconic products or buildings
- Before/after comparisons
- Stock charts or data (if relevant)

## A/B Testing Approach

1. **Create 2-3 thumbnail variants** per video
2. **Test with YouTube's thumbnail test feature** (if available) or swap after 24hrs
3. **Measure**: CTR (click-through rate), impressions, views
4. **Keep the winner**, learn from the loser
5. **Track patterns**: What styles/colors/text get highest CTR per niche

## Thumbnail Workflow

1. **Extract hook** from script — the core claim or dramatic fact
2. **Choose 3-5 word text** that captures the hook
3. **Select imagery** — photo, logo, or illustration related to topic
4. **Apply channel style** — colors, fonts, layout from this guide
5. **Compose** — text + image + accent on dark/clean background
6. **Test at small size** — can you read it at 168x94px?
7. **Verify accuracy** — text must match actual video content
8. **Export** — 1280x720, under 2MB, PNG or JPG

---

## Common Thumbnail Mistakes

1. **Too much text** — if you can't read it in 0.5 sec, it's too much
2. **Low contrast** — text blends into background
3. **Clickbait** — thumbnail promises something video doesn't deliver
4. **Inconsistent style** — channel looks unprofessional in feed
5. **Busy backgrounds** — competing visual elements distract
6. **Tiny text** — unreadable on mobile
7. **Wrong aspect ratio** — gets cropped poorly
8. **Generic stock photos** — signals low effort
9. **No emotional hook** — doesn't make you want to click
10. **Different style per video** — no brand recognition
