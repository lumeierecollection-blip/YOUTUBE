A nineteen seventy-one horror drama banned for decades returns in four thousand.

On October thirty, twenty twenty-six, BFI Distribution and Warner Bros. Clockwork released the four thousand restoration. The film stars Oliver Reed and Vanessa Redgrave as Urbain Grandier. How many years was it banned before twenty twenty-six?

Yet the film vanished from screens for over fifty years.

The restored cut runs one hundred fourteen minutes, preserving Ken Russell’s unflinching vision. Censors hid it for over fifty years, muting its brutal seventeenth‑century hysteria. Now audiences in the UK and Ireland can finally witness the masterpiece.

The Devils finally returns, restored in full four thousand glory.
