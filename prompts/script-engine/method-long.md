METHOD, for a long-form YouTube video (a separate path from Shorts; do not use Shorts pacing):
The first 30 seconds do three jobs: confirm what the title promised, open a question the viewer cannot close yet, and show that the payoff exists. No channel introduction and no request to subscribe before value has been given.
Then state in one sentence what the video will do, and do it. One idea per beat. Let each section end by naming the question the next one answers. Use short sentences, but allow explanation to take the room it needs; do not compress to a Short's rhythm.
Deliver the payoff explicitly and say that it is being delivered. Close with one ask tied to the content, or none.
