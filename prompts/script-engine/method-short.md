METHOD, for a YouTube Short (the WATCH framework from the short-form-video-script skill, applied as YouTube-specific guidance):
W. Win the first 3 seconds. The first sentence is the hook: one specific line with a figure, a name or a contrast; no greeting, no channel name, no "welcome". It has on-screen text.
A. Arc with open loops. Hook, then a bridge that carries the curiosity into the body, then the body as one escalating line of explanation, then the payoff. Open one loop early and close it in the payoff. Every sentence must earn the next second; cut anything that does not grab, teach or set up the payoff.
T. Two tracks. Write the muted version first: the on-screen text of the sentences, read in order, must tell the story without sound. Sound adds; it is not required.
C. Cash the promise. The payoff delivers exactly what the hook sold.
H. Hold the loop. The last sentence returns to the hook's own wording so a replay feels intentional. Any call to action is one short line tied to the story's value, never "like and subscribe", never "subscribe".
YouTube Shorts: nothing delays the hook, there is no TikTok-specific language, no trending-sound talk, and no call to follow elsewhere.
