On 21 October 1966, a coal-waste tip collapsed on the village of Aberfan, Wales, burying a school and killing 144 people, including 116 children.

Decades later, the National Library of Wales completed new copies of the Books of Remembrance, preserving all 144 names in leather-bound volumes ahead of July 2026.

The names live on, preserved for generations to come.

Sources: National Library of Wales (library.wales), BBC News (bbc.co.uk/news/uk-wales-36997227)
