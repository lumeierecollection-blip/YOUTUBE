# Gray Matter sample: story outline

Topic: What one night without sleep does to the hippocampus
Status: hand-planned sample, not from the script engine

Question: What is physically happening in the brain after a night without sleep?

Thesis: Sleep loss makes new learning harder, and in mice the mechanism runs through the loss of connection points on hippocampal neurons. Whether the same happens in people is not shown by this evidence.

- b1 (opening): Ties the everyday experience to a measured memory effect. [C1]
- b2 (context): Shows where in the brain the question leads. [C2]
- b3 (development): Human imaging evidence for a change in that region. [C3]
- b4 (development): Cellular change in mice. [C4, C5]
- b5 (payoff): The test that makes the mechanism causal in mice. [C6, C9]
- b6 (ending): Recovery, then what is and is not known. [C7, C8]
