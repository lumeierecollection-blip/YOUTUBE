# Saturn is losing its rings to ring rain

Why would Saturn's rings disappear, and how sure are we?

Dark bands on Saturn point to ring material raining into the planet, but how long the rings last, and how old they are, is still argued.

- b4 (development): Make the loss rate physical.
- b5 (payoff): Give the timescale and the scientific disagreement.
