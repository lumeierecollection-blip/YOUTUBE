# Story outline

Central question: How did Martha Stewart recover her career and media empire after her conviction in the ImClone stock trading case?

Thesis: Martha Stewart was convicted of felony charges and served five months in federal prison related to the ImClone stock trading case. Despite speculation that the incident would end her media empire, she successfully launched a comeback campaign in 2005, returned her company to profitability, and reclaimed leadership roles.

Why it matters: Understanding how a major public figure recovered from a federal conviction offers insight into crisis management and brand resilience.

Viewer ends understanding: The viewer understands the legal fallout of the ImClone case and the timeline of her successful career recovery.
