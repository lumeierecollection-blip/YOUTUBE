> not reviewed by the engine critic. written directly on the owner's instruction from fetched sources.

1. Can AI pick a random number?  
   claims: none
2. We asked two models for a number from one to ten, thirty times each.  
   claims: design
3. A fair pick lands on each number about three times.  
   claims: fair
4. Google's Flash-Lite said seven, thirty times out of thirty.  
   claims: top0
5. OpenAI's GPT-OSS said seven, thirty times.  
   claims: top1
6. A random pick would vary, but this did not.  
   claims: none
7. From one to a hundred, Google's Flash-Lite chose forty-two most often, twenty-six times.  
   claims: hundred0
8. People reportedly do it too: the blue-seven effect.  
   claims: blue7
9. For a fair draw, use dice, or ask AI to write code.
