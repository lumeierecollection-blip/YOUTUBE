# Blueprint

Central question: Can AI models accurately count letters?

Thesis: In a direct test, GPT‑OSS 120B answered every letter‑counting question correctly, while Gemini 3.5 Flash‑Lite got only 11 of 20 right, especially struggling with random strings.

Conclusion: GPT‑OSS 120B counts letters perfectly, while Gemini 3.5 Flash‑Lite falls short, so not all AI models are reliable at basic counting tasks.
