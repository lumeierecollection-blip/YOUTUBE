# Do AI models admit what they don't know?

> not reviewed by the engine critic. written directly on the owner's instruction from fetched sources.

   claims: none
2. We asked each model about ten made-up products.  
   claims: design
3. Then ten real questions, as a control.  
   claims: design
4. Google's Gemini Flash admitted it on 7 of ten.  
   claims: best
5. OpenAI's GPT-OSS admitted it on 2 of ten.  
   claims: worst
6. It described a product that does not exist.  
   claims: miss
