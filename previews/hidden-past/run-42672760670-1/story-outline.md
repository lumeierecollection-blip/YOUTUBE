Central question: Does the famous Sarajevo arrest photograph actually show Gavrilo Princip?

Thesis: The photograph does not show Gavrilo Princip; scholars identify the man as Ferdinand Behr, and no authentic arrest picture of Princip exists.

Conclusion: The image does not depict Gavrilo Princip; it is Ferdinand Behr, and no authentic arrest photograph of Princip is known.
