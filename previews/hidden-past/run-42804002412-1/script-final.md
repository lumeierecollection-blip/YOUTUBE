The Berlin Wall opened after an announcement came too early.
He spoke live on East German television, 9 November 1989.
Spokesman Günter Schabowski had just been handed a note.
Asked when it began, he said: immediately, without delay.
West German news told viewers the borders were open.
That night, guards at Bornholmer Straße let people through.
One unprepared answer opened the checkpoints.
