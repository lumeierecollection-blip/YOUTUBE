Pablo Escobar's hippos are still alive, and Colombia has approved killing some of them.
In 1981, he imported four of them for his private zoo.
His estate lay between Medellín and Bogotá.
After he died in 1993, they escaped and spread.
By 2007 there were sixteen, by 2014 about forty, and now around two hundred.
In April 2026, Colombia approved killing more than eighty of them.
An Indian billionaire offered to take them to India instead.
Most conservationists call them invasive, but some ecologists say they should stay.
So who decides what happens to Escobar's hippos?
