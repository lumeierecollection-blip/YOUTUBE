## NEW ARCHITECTURE

### Flow (OpenCode-first)
`
Script + SRT
    ↓
OpenCode generates VISUAL INTENT (primary thinker)
    ↓
Confidence gate: >=80% high-confidence beats?
    ├─ YES → Skip Gemini challenge (0 calls)
    └─ NO → Gemini challenges intent (1 call)
              ├─ MATCH → Proceed to render
              └─ NEEDS_CHANGE → Apply deltas, re-challenge (max 1 iteration)
    ↓
Convert intent → visual-plan.json
    ↓
Render with Remotion
    ↓
Compare execution vs intent (deterministic)
    ↓
Local audit (deterministic)
    ├─ NORMAL → Skip Gemini post-render review (0 calls)
    └─ MEDIUM/HIGH → Gemini post-render review (1 call)
`

### Token Budget
- **Normal video (high confidence + NORMAL audit):** 0 Gemini calls
- **Challenged video:** 1 Gemini call (challenge only)
- **Risky video:** 1-2 Gemini calls (challenge + post-render review)
- **Old flow:** 2+ Gemini calls per video (plan + review)
- **Savings:** 50-100% reduction in Gemini usage
